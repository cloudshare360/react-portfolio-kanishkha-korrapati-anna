# Employer-focused portfolio research

Research date: June 19, 2026.

## Evidence

- NN/g eye-tracking research shows that users scan pages, with attention concentrated near the top and left when hierarchy is weak. The design implication is to front-load role fit, quantified evidence, and descriptive headings rather than relying on introductory prose. Source: [NN/g — F-Shaped Pattern of Reading](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/).
- Financial-analyst portfolio guidance recommends showing achievements, credentials, role, skills used, organizational impact, clear navigation, and visual project evidence. Source: [Noble Desktop — Financial Analyst Portfolio Guide](https://www.nobledesktop.com/careers/financial-analyst/portfolio-tips).
- Data-analytics portfolio reviews emphasize that strong projects alone are insufficient; presentation, communication, personality, and personal brand affect how work is understood. Source: [CareerFoundry — Data Analytics Portfolio Examples](https://careerfoundry.com/en/blog/data-analytics/data-analytics-portfolio-examples/).
- Recruiter guidance frames a portfolio as tangible evidence that a candidate can perform the target role. Source: [Hunter Recruiting — Standout Portfolio Website](https://www.hirecruiting.com/newsroom/how-to-create-a-standout-portfolio-website/).
- WCAG 2.2 requires adaptable orientation, text alternatives, keyboard operation, visible focus, and at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text. Source: [W3C — How to Meet WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/).
- Core Web Vitals assess loading, interaction, and visual stability through LCP, INP, and CLS, evaluated separately across mobile and desktop. Source: [web.dev — Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals).

## Applied decisions

1. Keep the opening statement, target discipline, resume action, portrait, and four strongest metrics above the fold.
2. Add two verified case studies before the chronological experience section. Each shows the decision question, analysis, tools, scope, and delivered communication.
3. Preserve confidentiality instead of inventing downloadable work samples.
4. Declare portrait dimensions to reduce layout shift and prioritize the hero image.
5. Add explicit desktop, tablet, and phone layouts; maintain 44px mobile interaction targets and respect reduced-motion preferences.
6. Keep navigation shallow and section headings descriptive for fast scanning.

## Next evidence upgrades

The highest-value future improvement is adding sanitized artifacts: one redacted model screenshot, one Power BI view, and one fixed-income recommendation excerpt. These should be published only with employer/fund permission and explanatory captions.
